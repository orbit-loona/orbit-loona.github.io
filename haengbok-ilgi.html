Taeri TV "Happiness Diary" Archive

Taeri TV (Korean: 태Tae리ritv) is a YouTube channel run by a South Korean senior aerobics instructor named Jin-hee Yoon (Korean: 윤Yoon진Jin희Hee). Her streams feature dancing to trot (a musical genre from Korea) and trot remixes as well as old Korean music.
This page collects the entries of the "Happiness Diary" (Korean: 행복Haengbog-일기ilgi) which she posts daily on her public YouTube community to inspire happiness. The images on nearly eveery post are not included, because YouTube makes them more difficult to scrape, but can be viewed by clicking the link to the original post on YouTube.


These entries are all in Korean. (Using the back button will leave the page. This is because the history spam would be intolerable otherwise.)

Many entries are missing.
